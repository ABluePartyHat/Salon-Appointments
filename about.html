YOUR STYLIST, GLADYS BRITO

A love for hair.
A heart for people.

I believe your best hair starts with being heard.

My story began in Cuba. When I came here as an immigrant, I carried a love of beauty and a determination to build something meaningful. Hair became my way of helping people feel confident, cared for, and connected.

Over the last 20+ years, I’ve learned that the smallest details often make the biggest difference: a little warmth in your color, the way a layer falls, the time we take to talk before the scissors come out.

My chair is a place to settle in, be yourself, and leave feeling a little lighter.
