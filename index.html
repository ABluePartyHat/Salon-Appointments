My journey began in Cuba. As an immigrant, I brought my love of hair, my work ethic, and a dream of creating a space where every person feels truly seen. More than 20 years later, that’s still at the heart of everything I do.

From lived-in color to a beautifully tailored cut, we’ll find the look that belongs to you.
